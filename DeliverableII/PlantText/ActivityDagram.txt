@startuml
title Activity Diagram - Plan Trip and Generate Itinerary

|User|
start
:Open app and log in;
:Fill trip form\n(origin, destination, dates,\nbudget, preferences, transport mode);
:Click "Plan Trip";

|Frontend|
:Send TripRequest to backend;

|Backend|
:Validate TripRequest;
:Geocode destination;
:Fetch weather forecast;
:Fetch flights (if requested);
:Fetch driving route (if requested);
:Fetch Points of Interest;
:Fetch currency rate;
:Request Open AI summary of TripPlan;
:Return summary to frontend;

|User|
:Review trip summary;
if (Satisfied with plan?) then (yes)
  :Click "Generate Itinerary";
  |Frontend|
  :Send ItineraryRequest to backend;
  |Backend|
  :Load trip and preferences;
  :Refresh or reuse supporting data;
  :Call Open ai to build itinerary;
  :Save itinerary in MongoDB;
  :Return itinerary to frontend;
  |User|
  :View day-by-day itinerary;
else (no)
  :Adjust dates or preferences;
  :Re-run Plan Trip;
endif
stop

@enduml
